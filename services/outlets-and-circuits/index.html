An extra receptacle does not establish extra capacity

Receptacle locations and circuit design answer different questions. More convenient plug-in points may share the same existing circuit. New appliances or specialty equipment may need a different connection, a dedicated circuit or other assessed work. Give the Licensed Electrical Contractor the equipment information and intended location so the proposal follows the actual use.

Prepare the scope for your Aurora property

List each device in the room by name and note which ones run all day, which run only now and then and which sit in a damp or dusty spot. Photograph the spot where each device would plug in.

Ask the contractor which of the listed devices need a circuit of their own and how the proposal treats any that may stay on the shared one. Ask what protection the damp locations need.

What should the assessment establish?

When a quote names a new circuit for the sump pump and says nothing of the circuit the pump uses now, you cannot tell what else shares it. Everything on that circuit today is listed in the scope, with the devices that move to new circuits and those that stay. Each new receptacle has its position and the equipment it is meant to serve. Where a business or hobby adds computers, heaters or tools in an Aurora home, the contractor explains how their combined use was considered. Damp or exposed positions come with a note on how they are treated.

Ontario electrical safety

ESA says almost all electrical work needs a notification of work. If you hire someone for home electrical work, use a Licensed Electrical Contractor. Agree who files the notification and request the Certificate of Acceptance after ESA review.

Read the current ESA notification and inspection guidance, contractor hiring guidance. You can verify an electrical contractor with ESA before agreeing to work.

Questions before you agree to work

Does a sump pump really need a separate circuit from my other equipment?

The equipment and the existing circuit decide it, and the contractor must assess both. Sharing can matter because a pump that stops when the circuit trips may go unnoticed. Ask for a clear answer in writing.

What is wrong with a power strip as the fix for too few plug-in points?

A strip adds plug-in points but not circuit capacity, so several long-running devices on one strip can make it and its receptacle run warm. Ask the contractor where permanent receptacles should go and which devices should have their own circuit.
